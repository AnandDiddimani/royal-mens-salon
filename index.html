About Us

Your neighbourhood
grooming destination.

Located right by the Rajapalya bus stop in Krishnarajapuram, Royal Men's Beauty Salon is where the men of Basavanna Nagar come for a fresh look and a great experience.

We combine skilled barbering with clean, hygienic tools and quality products to deliver haircuts, beard styling, colour and facials that keep you looking your best — all at honest, affordable prices. Walk-ins welcome.
